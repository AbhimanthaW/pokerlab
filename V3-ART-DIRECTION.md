# V3 art direction

The Night Room is a contained, original low-poly lounge: navy architectural walls, brown wainscoting, muted green felt, a padded dark rail, brass inlays, warm pendant lamps, geometric framed artwork and restrained plants. It remains exclusively Hold’em.

The room, table and seats are assembled from authored geometry. Characters have modeled heads, eyes, pupils, brows, noses, mouths, ears, hair, necks, layered clothing, buttons, articulated arms, hands and seated legs/chairs. Five appearance profiles vary skin, shirt/vest palette, hair and face proportions, with a beard, glasses or cap on selected avatars. Appearance is independent of betting personality. A separate dealer outfit and pose identify the non-playing dealer.

Cards use original locally generated canvas faces and patterned backs on thin physical meshes. Rank/suit values come from the authoritative public projection. Generic system serif fonts are rasterized locally; no proprietary font files are distributed. Chip colors indicate 100/25/5/1 denominations; capped token stacks illustrate quantity while the HUD shows exact totals.

The HTML interface uses ivory and muted gold, small seat labels, compact actions and temporary overlays. There is no global dashboard sidebar in Play. Music is a subdued original synthesized instrumental with chordal keyboard, plucked-bass-like tones and quiet brushed noise percussion; no sampled or commercial music is used.

Camera comfort, lighting balance, avatar readability, clipping, label placement, small-mobile control overlap and auditory quality require the manual/browser gate described in V3-QA-REPORT.md.
